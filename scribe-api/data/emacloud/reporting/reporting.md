# Campaign Reporting

Review full campaign analytics to evaluate performance and conversion metrics.

## Key Statistics

- Open Rate: Percentage of recipients who opened your email.
- Click-Through Rate (CTR): Clicks on links within the email body.
- Bounce Rate: Hard or soft delivery failures.
- Unsubscribe Rate: Recipients opt-outs.
